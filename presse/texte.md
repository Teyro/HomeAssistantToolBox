## KDE Store (store.kde.org) – Beschreibung / description

Home Assistant ToolBox shows your Home Assistant lights, sockets, heating, energy and people right in the panel –
in the familiar Breeze look.

[b]Features[/b]
- Lights: groups and rooms, switch and brightness, icons in the real light colour, “All off”
- Sockets: power strips with shared switch and total consumption
- Energy: current consumption, 24 h chart, today’s electricity/water/gas, biggest consumers
- Heating: temperature and humidity per room, target temperature, mode, preset, boost, window open/closed, 24 h chart
- People: map with zones, who is where
- Several Home Assistant instances, desktop tiles, live updates via WebSocket, built‑in updater
- 8 languages: English, Deutsch, 中文, हिन्दी, Español, العربية, Français, বাংলা

[b]Setup[/b]
Right‑click the icon → Configure → enter your Home Assistant address and a long‑lived access token.
Optional for live updates: Qt WebSockets (qt6-websockets / qml6-module-qtwebsockets).

Also available for macOS and iPhone: https://github.com/Teyro/HomeAssistantToolBox
Not affiliated with Home Assistant / Nabu Casa / Open Home Foundation.

**Title:** I made Home Assistant ToolBox – Home Assistant in the KDE panel, the macOS menu bar and on iPhone (free, open source)

Hi everyone! I wanted quick access to my Home Assistant without opening a browser tab, so I built
**Home Assistant ToolBox**:

- **KDE Plasma 6 widget** in the Breeze look (panel + desktop tiles)
- **macOS menu bar app** with Liquid Glass design and desktop widgets
- **iPhone/iPad widgets** via Scriptable (battery friendly: one request per refresh)

Lights (groups/rooms, brightness), sockets with power usage, energy (24 h chart, today’s electricity/water/gas),
heating (target temperature, boost, window open, 24 h chart), people with map, multiple instances.
Live via WebSocket, no cloud, 8 languages, GPL‑3.0.

Screenshots and downloads: https://github.com/Teyro/HomeAssistantToolBox

Feedback and translation fixes are very welcome!
