# Press release

## Home Assistant ToolBox 1.0 puts your smart home into the KDE panel, the Mac menu bar and on your iPhone

*Free software (GPL-3.0), no cloud, no account – in eight languages*

**October 2026** – **Home Assistant ToolBox 1.0** is a free tool that brings the popular open‑source smart home hub
[Home Assistant](https://www.home-assistant.io/) to where people work every day: as a **KDE Plasma 6 widget** next to
the clock, as a **macOS menu bar app** in the Liquid Glass design of macOS 26, and as **iPhone and iPad widgets** via
the Scriptable app. All three versions share the same logic and the same features.

One click is enough to switch lights and sockets, turn up the heating in the living room, check the current power
consumption or see who is at home – without a browser tab and without switching apps.

### Highlights

- **Lights**: groups and rooms, switch and brightness for every light, icons in the real light colour, “All off”
- **Sockets**: power strips as one row with shared switch and total consumption
- **Energy**: current consumption, 24‑hour history, today’s electricity, water and gas (straight from the Home
  Assistant energy dashboard), biggest consumers
- **Heating**: temperature and humidity per room, target temperature, mode, preset, boost for 30 minutes to 4 hours,
  window‑open indicator and 24‑hour chart
- **People**: map with zones, who is where and how far from home
- **Several Home Assistant installations**, e.g. home and holiday house
- **Desktop widgets** (KDE and macOS) plus home‑screen and lock‑screen widgets (iOS)
- **Live updates** via WebSocket, **one‑click updates**
- **Eight languages**: English, German, Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, Arabic (right‑to‑left), French and Bengali

### Privacy

Home Assistant ToolBox talks only to the user’s own Home Assistant. No cloud, no account, no tracking. On the Mac and
on iOS credentials are stored in the system keychain.

### Availability

Home Assistant ToolBox 1.0 is available now, free of charge, on GitHub:
**https://github.com/Teyro/HomeAssistantToolBox**

- KDE Plasma 6 (any distribution, e.g. Arch, Manjaro, Fedora, openSUSE, Kubuntu)
- macOS 14 or later (Apple Silicon and Intel)
- iPhone and iPad with the free Scriptable app
